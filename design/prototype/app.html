The whole customer journey, UC-01 to UC-04. Tap through it in Flow, or switch to All screens for one board you can put on a slide.

Two moments carry the architecture. On Held, VIP drops from 16 to 14 before any money is discussed, which is why the same seat cannot be sold twice. On Hold expired, the first and largest thing on the screen is that no money was taken. Press Let the hold expire to see it.
